Fenton Moore's Management Framework (v1.1)

Fenton Moore's Management Framework v1.1 is a puzzle/mystery game taking place entirely within the command terminal of an old digital assistant.

It quickly becomes clear that things are more than they seem when the simple tool suddenly transforms into a strange, half-built RPG… and a strategy game… and a pong-like… and more.

As you play you gain access to more and more new commands, commands that can be used in new contexts, which can make other games easier or even trivial if used cleverly.

When your character dies the program is reset.

Try to make sense of the strange program, and unravel the mystery of what happened to Fenton Moore.
