Sunken Eyes

Sunken Eyes is a survival horror game following the crew of a mining site in Alaska, harvesting a rare mineral with supernatural properties.

Navigate the winding tunnels of RQF-1, wading through floods, storming through fires, shoving through cave-ins and breaking down doors to try and save as many of the ten crew members as you can. Fortunately there's lots of resources that should make getting out a piece of cake, but be careful-everything you do effects the others. If you're reckless it can be easy to deprive other characters of crucial resources (even once you're finished with them).

Make sure to keep your stress low or you'll start to hallucinate... or maybe not. It's hard to believe you're hallucinating when the phantoms cut a gash in your thigh or blow up the room you're in. Regardless-things always seem to go better when you keep an even head.

Whenever a character successfully escapes (or tragically passes), you headhop into the next. This continues until the facility is empty. All characters have different toolsets, knowledge, abilities and (most importantly) stress triggers.
