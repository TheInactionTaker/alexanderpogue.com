Forest's Bane

Forest's Bane is a survival horror-roguelite where you play as Wesley Lockhart, a radical conspiracy theorist unearthing the mysteries of an island shrouded in darkness.

Manage your hunger, energy, morale, items, Wesley's obsession and the many cryptids wandering the dark forests to survive the expedition and make it home safe.

Unlock new artifacts, storylines and even playable characters as you progress, and attempt to answer the many questions of the island.
